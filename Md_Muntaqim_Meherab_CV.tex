\section*{Research Profile}
My research focuses on robust and computationally efficient computer vision for remote sensing and climate applications. Recent work examines mid-layer representations in frozen vision transformers, predictive confidence under cloud occlusion in flood segmentation, and robustness to noisy or missing inputs. My broader aim is to improve the reliability of vision models under limited compute and imperfect observations. I emphasize controlled evaluation and reproducible implementations.
